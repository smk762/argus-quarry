The question

Was this colour photo taken on a digital camera, or is it a scan of film (a negative, slide or print)? Judge the capture, not the subject: a modern photo of an old car is digital.

Use Can't tell freely. A guess counts against the measurement more than a skip does.

Film scan signs

  • Grain at full size: irregular clumps, even in flat sky, with no blocky pattern
  • Dust, hairs, scratches, or a film edge, frame number or rebate in the scan
  • Faded or shifted colour (magenta, cyan or yellow casts) across the whole frame
  • Halation glow around bright lights; soft, rolled-off highlights
  • Print texture or paper sheen; a white border

Digital signs

  • Clean, smooth flat areas; fine colour noise in shadows rather than grain
  • Clipped, hard-edged highlights; heavy sharpening halos
  • Very shallow depth of field on a small sensor look, or HDR-style local contrast
  • Screens, phones, LED lighting or other post-2000 subjects (only supporting evidence)
